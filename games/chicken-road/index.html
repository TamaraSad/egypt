How Chicken Road works

  1. Choose your stake and a difficulty level.
  2. Tap to move the chicken forward one lane at a time.
  3. Each lane it crosses safely raises the multiplier.
  4. Cash out at any point to take stake × the current multiplier.
  5. If the chicken gets hit, the round ends and the stake is lost.
