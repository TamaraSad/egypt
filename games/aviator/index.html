How Aviator works

  1. Place one or two bets during the short countdown before take-off.
  2. The plane takes off and the multiplier grows from 1.00x.
  3. Cash out at any moment: your win is your stake × the multiplier at that moment.
  4. If the plane flies away before you cash out, that bet is lost.
  5. The crash point of each round is random, and you can verify it with the provably fair check.
