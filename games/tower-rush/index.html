How Tower Rush works

  1. Set your stake before the round starts.
  2. The crane starts stacking floors on the tower.
  3. Every new floor raises the multiplier.
  4. Cash out at any moment to take stake × the current multiplier.
  5. If the tower collapses before you cash out, the stake is lost.
