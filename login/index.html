How to log in to 1xBet on the website

Takes less than a minute on your phone or computer.

  1. Open the official 1xBet site

    Type the address yourself or use a saved bookmark — not a link from a chat or ad.

  2. Tap “Log in”

    You’ll find it at the top right of the page.

  3. Choose how to sign in

    ID, phone number or email — the one you registered with.

  4. Enter your password

    Use the eye icon to check it for typos.

  5. Confirm if asked

    Enter the SMS or authenticator code if two-step verification is on.

3 ways to sign in to 1xBet

Pick the tab that matches how you registered. Using the wrong tab is the most common reason a correct password “doesn’t work”.

Account ID + password

Your 1xBet account number from registration — handy if you signed up with one click.

Phone number + password

1xBet login with phone number: enter your +234 number and password. An SMS code may be requested.

Email + password

Use the email you registered with. Good if you don’t want to depend on SMS.

1xBet login in the app

  1. Open the 1xBet app — see the app guide if you don’t have it yet.
  2. Tap Log in on the first screen.
  3. Choose ID, phone or email and enter your password.
  4. Tick Remember me on your own phone only.

1xBet forgot password: how to reset it

  1. Tap Forgot password? on the login form.
  2. Choose SMS or email and enter your phone number or email.
  3. Enter the code you receive.
  4. Create a new password you don’t use anywhere else.

1xBet login problems and how to fix them

Wrong password

Check the login tab, Caps Lock and the +234 prefix. Still failing? Reset the password.

SMS code not arriving

Check your signal and the number, wait a minute and request a new code, or reset by email.

Account blocked

Too many wrong attempts or a security check. Contact support from the official site — not through social media.

Site not opening

Check your internet connection and the address, clear the browser cache, or use the official app.

Logged out all the time

Allow cookies for the site, keep the app updated and don’t use private browsing.

Verification requested

1xBet may ask you to confirm your identity. Upload a clear photo of a valid ID from your account settings.

Keep your 1xBet account safe

  • Turn on two-step verification in your account settings — a code is needed at every login.
  • Use a unique password that you don’t use for email or social media.
  • Never share SMS codes — 1xBet support will never ask for them.
  • Log out on shared devices and don’t tick “Remember me” there.
  • Check your login history in the account settings from time to time.

Watch out for fake login pages: scammers copy the 1xBet sign-in page and share it in WhatsApp groups, Telegram and ads. Always check the address in your browser before typing your password, or use the official app.

1xBet login FAQ

How do I log in to 1xBet Nigeria?

Tap “Log in” at the top of the official 1xBet site or on the first screen of the app, enter your account ID, phone number or email and your password, then tap “Log in”.

Can I log in to 1xBet with my phone number?

Yes. Choose the phone option, enter your Nigerian number with +234 and your password. 1xBet may also send an SMS code to confirm it’s you.

I forgot my 1xBet password. What should I do?

Tap “Forgot password?” on the login form and reset it by SMS or email. You need access to the phone number or email linked to your account.

Why can’t I log in to 1xBet?

The most common reasons are a wrong password, the wrong login method selected, a typo in the phone number or a fake site. Check the address, reset your password, and contact support from the official site if your account is blocked.

Is it safe to log in to 1xBet from links in WhatsApp or Telegram?

No. Fake sites copy the 1xBet login page to steal accounts. Type the address yourself or use the official app.

Can I use the same login in the app and on the website?

Yes. Your 1xBet login details work on the app, the mobile site and the desktop site.
